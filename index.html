Data availability

Historical coverage: 2021–present

The server provides electricity spot-price data from 1 January 2021 through today. This includes historical periods from 2021 onward, plus the latest available data for the current year. Ask Claude for a specific zone, date range, average, or negative-price event.

Nordic bidding zones

DK1DK2DESE3SE4NO2

⬇️ Negative pricing

Negative prices occur when generation (often wind or solar) exceeds demand and inflexible plants pay to offload power. This server tracks these events across Nordic zones in detail — frequency, duration, and affected hours.

Who this is for

  • Academic researchers studying electricity market design, renewable integration, or demand flexibility in Nordic markets
  • Industry analysts monitoring price dynamics and arbitrage opportunities across Nordic bidding zones
  • Energy economists building models that require clean, structured historical Nordic price data
  • Policy researchers examining the effects of high renewable penetration on Nordic market prices

Connect to Claude

Add this MCP server to Claude in three steps — no coding required.

1

Open Claude Settings

Go to claude.ai → click your profile icon (top right) → Settings → Connectors.

2

Add a new MCP server

Click Add connector, then paste this URL:

https://remote-mcp-server-1gid.onrender.com/mcp
3

Start asking questions

Once connected, ask things like:
"Show me negative pricing hours in DK1 for 2023" or "What was the average spot price in SE3 between 2021 and today?"
